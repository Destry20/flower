30 Congratulations Messages to Write in a Card

Sometimes it's hard to find the right words to celebrate someone properly. Here are 30 ready-made lines: universal ones for any occasion, words for a specific achievement or success, and a few with a bit of humor for people you can tease a little.

Universal

  • Congratulations! May this be just the beginning of even better things.
  • Congratulations from the bottom of my heart — you earned this.
  • Congrats on this big moment! May many more follow.
  • So happy for you — may your luck keep going strong.
  • Congratulations! You just proved persistence always pays off.
  • May this moment bring you pride, and many more after it.
  • Congratulations — you handled this brilliantly!
  • Congrats on this wonderful moment! May the joy from it last a long time.
  • Moments like this deserve to be celebrated — congratulations, truly.
  • Congratulations! May everything ahead be even better.

For an Achievement or Success

  • Congratulations on this well-earned success — you worked hard for it.
  • Your effort and persistence finally paid off — congratulations!
  • Congrats on this win! You proved to yourself what you're capable of.
  • So proud of you — this achievement is entirely your own doing.
  • Congratulations on reaching a new level! Only up from here.
  • You did it! Congratulations on a result that wasn't easy to reach.
  • Congrats on this success — may it be the first of many.
  • Your determination is impressive — congratulations on a well-deserved win.
  • Congratulations! Every step it took to get here was worth it.
  • May this achievement fuel you for whatever's next. Congratulations!

With a Bit of Humor

  • Congrats! You now officially have bragging rights at every gathering.
  • Finally! Congratulations — and yes, you can tell this story however flatters you most.
  • Congrats! A little luck, a little talent, and a lot of stubbornness — great combo.
  • You did it — now you can relax... at least until the next goal shows up.
  • Congratulations! Some will call it luck, but we know how much work was behind it.
  • Officially declaring: you're impressive. Congrats on the result!
  • Congratulations! Time to treat yourself — you earned it, even if you won't admit it.
  • Finally! Congrats, I was starting to wonder if this day would ever come.
  • Congratulations! Now you get to casually bring this up in conversation forever.
  • An achievement like this can't go unnoticed — congrats, and I want all the details!
