30 Birthday Wishes to Write in a Card

Sometimes the hardest part isn't picking a gift — it's finding the right words. Here are 30 ready-made wishes: heartfelt ones, short ones for when space is tight, and a few with a bit of humor for the people you can tease. Use them as they are, or as a starting point for your own.

Heartfelt & Warm

  • May this year bring you more good days than hard ones, and people around you who see exactly how much you're worth.
  • Happy birthday — I hope every year makes you a little braver and a little kinder to yourself.
  • You're one of those rare people who make everything around them a bit lighter. Here's to more of that this year.
  • I hope this year quietly brings you the thing you've been wishing for and never said out loud.
  • Happy birthday to someone who deserves warmth, good company, and real laughter — not just today, but all year.
  • Wishing you a year with fewer things to worry about and more reasons to be proud of yourself.
  • May every step you take this year feel a little more certain, and may there always be someone nearby to lean on.
  • You deserve happiness without conditions attached. I hope this year gives you exactly that.
  • Happy birthday — may this year bring new people worth knowing and old friendships that only get stronger.
  • I hope you wake up this year feeling like life is actually on your side.

Short & Sweet

  • Happy birthday — you've earned every bit of happiness coming your way.
  • Wishing you a kind year ahead.
  • Happy birthday! More reasons to smile than to worry.
  • Grow, bloom, thrive. Happy birthday.
  • Hope everything you're planning actually works out.
  • Happy birthday — you keep getting better with time, and it shows.
  • Warmth, love, and good spirits for the year ahead.
  • May this be the start of a genuinely good year.
  • Happy birthday — don't forget how much you matter.
  • Health, energy, and ease in everything you do.

With a Little Humor

  • Happy birthday — still pretending to be a functioning adult, I see.
  • Congrats on more proof that, like a good wine, you just keep improving.
  • Happy birthday! May the cake be big and the candle count be slightly less terrifying this year.
  • Another year of wisdom... or at least more practice faking it.
  • Happy birthday — I promise not to mention your age if you promise to laugh at my jokes anyway.
  • Congratulations, you now have an official excuse to eat cake for breakfast.
  • Happy birthday — a year older and, hopefully, at least a little wiser. Kidding, you were already great.
  • May luck find you faster than you find excuses to procrastinate this year.
  • Happy birthday! Hope you get more presents than relatives asking when you're getting married.
  • Another trip around the sun — you're doing it with style.
