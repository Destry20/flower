26 Get-Well-Soon Messages for a Card

When someone you care about isn't feeling well, sometimes it's enough to just let them know you're thinking of them. Here are 26 lines: warm words of support, short wishes for when you'd rather keep it simple, and a few with a smile to help lift their mood.

Warm Words

  • I heard you're not feeling well — I really hope you feel better soon.
  • Rest as much as you need to. Everything else can wait, your health can't.
  • May each day feel a little easier than the last, and your strength come back faster than you expect.
  • I'm here, even if I can't be there in person — just tell me what you need.
  • Hoping your medicine helps, your sleep is deep, and your recovery is quick.
  • You've gotten through harder things than this — you'll get through this too.
  • Take care of yourself, allow yourself to rest, and don't rush your body — it's already doing its best.
  • Thinking of you, and looking forward to hearing you're feeling better.
  • Hoping this passes quickly and doesn't come back again this year.

Short and Simple

  • Get well soon!
  • Hang in there, you've got this.
  • Feel better soon — we miss having you around.
  • Sending you health and strength right now.
  • Rest up and get well — nothing else matters more right now.
  • Hoping tomorrow feels lighter.
  • Wishing you strength and patience.
  • You'll get through this — just give yourself time.
  • Get well soon, we're waiting to have you back in full form.

With a Smile

  • Get well soon — the shows aren't the same without you to watch them with.
  • Official permission to do nothing and lie around — use it fully.
  • Feel better — those germs clearly didn't know who they were messing with.
  • Get well soon — you never had time for bed rest anyway.
  • Hang in there, champion of the sick note. You're almost at the finish line.
  • Feel better soon — everyone's being suspiciously well-behaved without you.
  • Hoping the medicine isn't too bitter and the soup is actually good.
  • Get well soon! Sick leave isn't the vacation you wanted, but since you've got it, rest up properly.
