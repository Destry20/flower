24 Words of Sympathy for When You Don't Know What to Say

There's no perfect thing to say in moments like this — and that's okay. You don't need to find something profound; just showing up matters. Here are simple, sincere lines for three situations: when words won't come at all, when you want to offer real, practical help, and when you're writing to a colleague or someone you don't know well. Use a line as it is, or let it be a starting point for your own words.

Simple and Sincere

  • I'm so sorry for your loss. I'm here if you need me.
  • My deepest condolences. Please take care of yourself.
  • There are no words that could fix this — but I'm right here.
  • I'm grieving with you. You're not alone in this.
  • Thinking of you and your family during this difficult time.
  • My heart goes out to you.

When Words Are Hard to Find

  • I don't know what to say to make this easier — but I'm here.
  • There aren't enough words to say how sorry I am.
  • Sometimes just being near says more than words ever could — so I'm here.
  • I wish I had the right words. For now, just know I'm thinking of you.
  • Please accept my condolences — and know you have people who care.
  • May their memory bring you comfort in the hardest moments ahead.

Offering Real Support

  • If you need help with anything — errands, calls, anything at all — just say the word.
  • I'm here any time, day or night, if you want to talk or just sit in silence.
  • I'll stop by this week — will you let me bring you a cup of tea?
  • I won't say "let me know if you need anything" — I'm already on my way, just tell me when.
  • Let me take a few things off your plate, even small ones.
  • Cry, be angry, go quiet — whatever you need. I'm not going anywhere.

For a Colleague or Acquaintance

  • Please accept my sincere condolences on your loss.
  • I'm so sorry for your grief. If you need time or support, please let us know.
  • On behalf of the team, our deepest sympathy — we're here if you need anything.
  • May their memory always be a comfort to you.
  • We're thinking of you and your family.
  • If you need time off, please don't hesitate to ask — we'll take care of it.
