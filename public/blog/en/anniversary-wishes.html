27 Wedding Anniversary Wishes to Write in a Card

An anniversary is more than an occasion for a gift — it's a moment to look back at the road you've walked together. Here are 27 wishes: heartfelt ones that remember how it all started, short ones for when you'd rather keep it brief, and a few with a smile for couples who've been at it for a while.

Heartfelt Wishes

  • Happy anniversary — may this marriage keep as much laughter in it as the day you first met.
  • Another year together, and it keeps feeling more and more like home.
  • May your bond keep growing the way it always has — quietly, but for real.
  • Happy anniversary! Wishing you the same attentiveness to the small things you had at the very beginning.
  • May every anniversary you celebrate be a reminder of how lucky you are to have each other.
  • Happy anniversary! May everyday life never wear down the warmth you started with.
  • May there always be room for tenderness in your home, even years down the line.
  • Happy anniversary — may you keep choosing each other, as deliberately as you did the first time.
  • May this date be more than a line on the calendar — a reason to remember why you love each other.

Short Wishes

  • Happy anniversary — another year, another chapter in your story.
  • Wishing you love and patience for many years to come.
  • Happy anniversary! May it feel like day one, only better.
  • Another lap around the sun, still together. That's beautiful.
  • Happy anniversary — take care of each other.
  • May the years add up without you losing count of the happy moments.
  • Happy anniversary to two people who never stopped choosing each other.
  • Another year of proof that you're a team.
  • Happy anniversary — may it be easy and warm.

With a Smile — for the Long Haul

  • Happy anniversary — you're still together, so somebody's clearly very patient.
  • Another year without a divorce — that's an achievement worth a bouquet.
  • Congratulations on officially surviving each other for 365 more days.
  • Happy anniversary! The secret to a long marriage: half-listen, but love with your whole heart.
  • May this year bring just enough arguments to make the making-up worth it.
  • Happy anniversary! You're not really a couple anymore, you're a well-run team with shared Wi-Fi and shared dinner plans.
  • Another year, and you still find things to talk about — that's a rare gift.
  • Happy anniversary — may you never get tired of sharing one blanket.
  • Congratulations, you've made it through another year and still haven't filed the paperwork. That's love.
